📜 Coin Usage Rules

🟢 Free Coins

Free Coins can be used only to send RYZON gifts. They cannot be used for Premium, other app features, transfers, shopping, vouchers, recharge or bill payments.

🟡 Purchased Coins

Purchased Coins may be used for gifts, Premium and eligible app features, and transfers to accepted friends. They are separate from Free Coins and cannot be withdrawn as cash.

🛍️ Future shopping rewards

Shopping vouchers, recharge and bill-payment options are not active. Any future launch depends on approved providers, legal and accounting review, settlement and refund support.
