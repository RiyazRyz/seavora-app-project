Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create a RYZON account?

Open the login page and choose Email Sign Up or Mobile Sign Up. Follow the verification steps for your selected method.

Is Worldwide Random Chat free?

Yes. RYZON Worldwide Random Chat is intended to be free for everyone.

Are photo and video sharing free?

Photo Sharing and Video Sharing are paid features. Check the Premium page for feature information.

How many friend requests are free?

The first 10 friend requests are free. Additional requests require paid access.

Can I send gifts without Premium Membership?

Yes. Free gifts are available to free users. Paid gifts can be purchased individually without subscribing to Premium Membership.

How do I reset my password?

Use the Forgot Password option on the RYZON login page and follow the instructions sent to your registered email.

Open Login Page
How do I report a user?

Use the Block and Report options where available. Actual report processing depends on the reporting backend being connected.

How do I change my account settings?

Open the Settings page to manage available account, privacy, language and notification preferences.

Open Settings
How do I install RYZON as an app?

Open RYZON in a supported browser. If installation is available, use the browser's Install App or Add to Home Screen option.
