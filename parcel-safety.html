Parcel Safety

Stay alert when receiving parcels

Parcel Safety Checklist

  • Check the sender and delivery details.
  • Do not share OTP with unknown callers.
  • Never scan unknown QR codes for refunds.
  • Do not pay unexpected delivery charges through suspicious links.
  • Check the package condition before accepting it.
  • Report suspicious parcels to the delivery company.
